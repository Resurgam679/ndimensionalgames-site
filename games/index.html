Jet Slalom

Crisp re-renders the whole game at your display's real pixel density — clean vector edges, no stair-stepping. Crunchy draws at the original internal resolution and blows it up with nearest-neighbour, keeping the fat authentic pixels. Sound is on by default; settings apply live, mid-run — sound included, and pressing M inside a game keeps this in sync. Esc returns here. Touch launches the thumb-controlled build of Area Flat 3 — drag to fly, the gun fires itself, laser and bomb under your right thumb. Jet Slalom takes touch in its normal build: put a thumb down anywhere and pull, like a stick. Gamepad works in both; the dot lights when one is live. Browsers only hand a pad over after a button press inside the game, so it wakes on your first input. ADRIFT carries its own settings, controls and co-op lobby, so it opens full-screen in its own page rather than in the cabinet; Esc pauses it, and the browser's back button returns here.
